# Odd One Out — CrazyGames Basic Launch submission pack

Prepared for zero-cost submission. Do not accept portal terms or submit until the owner approves the developer agreement/terms step.

## Game title
Odd One Out

## Short description
Spot the one symbol that is different before the clock runs out. The grid gets harder as your score climbs.

## Long description
Odd One Out is a fast visual-search challenge built for short, replayable sessions. Find the symbol that differs from the rest, tap it, and immediately face a harder grid. You have 30 seconds to build the highest score you can. The rules take seconds to learn, but the tighter visual differences make each run increasingly demanding.

## How to play
1. Start the 30-second run.
2. Scan the grid for the symbol that is different from the others.
3. Tap or click the odd symbol.
4. Correct answers increase your score and move you to a harder grid.
5. Keep going until time runs out, then try to beat your best score.

## Controls
Desktop: left-click the odd symbol.
Mobile/tablet: tap the odd symbol.
