How it works

  1. Pick one of the lightweight HTML5 games.
  2. Add your visible score if you want a specific target.
  3. Send the generated link. The recipient lands directly in the selected game.
  4. After their run, they can replay or send a challenge back.

All games run free in the browser. No account, install, subscription, or purchase is required.
